# Step 1: SEO Audit and Keyword Mapping

## Project

- Brand: Cube Properties Group
- Website: `https://www.cubeproperties.co.uk/`
- Business type: London-focused property services company
- Core services from current site content:
  - Guaranteed Rent
  - Full Property Management
  - Let Only / Tenant Find
  - HMO Setup
- Primary audiences:
  - Landlords
  - Tenants
  - Potential partners / collaborators

## Step 1 Goal

This document defines the keyword foundation for the full SEO rollout. It maps:

- what the business appears to offer
- who each page should target
- which keywords each page should be built around
- which terms should be avoided or deprioritized
- what content gaps must be fixed in the next steps

This is the keyword and intent baseline for all later SEO work.

## Current Site Audit Summary

### What is already good

- The site has clear service themes.
- The site already uses service language that matches landlord intent:
  - guaranteed rent
  - property management
  - tenant find
  - HMO setup
- The site already includes basic trust signals:
  - London address
  - phone number
  - email
  - ICO registration
  - company number
- The structure is simple and crawl-friendly for a static website.
- The live domain is already publicly accessible and indexed at least for the homepage and legal pages.

### What is weak right now

- Keyword targeting is broad, but not tightly mapped per page.
- Homepage copy is too short for strong search intent coverage.
- Several high-value service terms appear only briefly.
- Local SEO signals are present but underused.
- Page intent is not fully separated:
  - landlord intent
  - tenant intent
  - service intent
  - contact intent
- Some wording is too generic to compete for search:
  - "best real estate company in the UK"
  - "work with us"
- There is no visible keyword strategy for area-based searches across London.

### Important SEO reality

This site can be made technically strong and keyword-aligned, but ranking still depends on:

- competition in London property SERPs
- domain authority
- backlinks and citations
- Google Business Profile strength
- ongoing content and trust signals

SEO can make the site rank-ready. It cannot guarantee position 1.

## Search Intent Model

For this business, search demand is most likely to come from:

1. Landlords looking for income stability
2. Landlords looking for management help
3. Landlords looking for tenants
4. Property owners looking for HMO support
5. People searching the brand directly
6. Local users searching by service + London

The SEO strategy should prioritize commercial-intent searches first.

## Content Language Recommendations

These phrases match the current business model and should be used naturally in later steps:

- guaranteed rent for landlords in London
- fully managed property services across London
- tenant find and let only services
- hands-free property management
- compliant, professionally managed tenancies
- stable rental income with reduced day-to-day involvement
- landlord-focused property solutions

## Terms To Deprioritize

These should not be leading targets unless the business later adds matching service depth:

- property sales london
- homes for sale london
- buy property london
- commercial property london
- estate agency uk
- best real estate company in the uk

Reason: these terms do not strongly match the visible service offering on the site.

## High-Value Content Gaps Found In Step 1

These are the biggest SEO weaknesses to fix in upcoming steps:

1. No dedicated long-form service copy for each major landlord intent.
2. Homepage does not fully explain guaranteed rent in search-friendly language.
3. Landlord page has too little crawlable text.
4. Contact page can do more with local business relevance.
5. The site does not yet show a clear page ownership strategy for keywords.
6. There are no location-specific supporting pages yet.

## Strategic Direction For Step 2+

Based on this Step 1 audit, the best SEO direction is:

1. Position the brand around `guaranteed rent london` and `property management london`.
2. Use the Services page as the main supporting commercial page.
3. Use the Landlord page as the primary conversion page for landlord-intent searchers.
4. Strengthen local trust signals across homepage, contact, and footer.
5. Avoid diluting relevance with broad "real estate company" language.
